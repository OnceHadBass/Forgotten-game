# Stage 2: masonry and materials (brief)

Written 28 September 2026, after Nathan signed off the values pass (0.10.3) on his GPU. The route and its reasons are in `values-pass-brief.md`.

## What the values pass showed

- On a real screen, outside the beam you see only glow plants and lit kit. The fill is too faint on an ordinary monitor. **Decision:** raise it until room edges are just readable (the brief's original target).
- Runnels are just visible. Puddles aren't: no glancing light, no reflections. The values pass's specular ceiling squashed their glint. **Decision:** water gets strong glints (its own ceiling, tighter gloss), still no true reflections.
- Glow plants look good.

## Decisions (28 Sept)

- **Stone surfaces are made by script, not downloaded.** The texture sites (ambientCG, Poly Haven) are blocked from the build workspace. The stone is procedural, generated by a committed script, so it's ours outright: reproducible, no licences, and tunable per layer. Scans can still replace it later; the shader wouldn't change.
- **Two sign-offs:**
  - **2a:** stone surfaces, water and darks.
  - **2b:** the Blender wall kit, pillars, door frames, and rubble at the joins.

## 2a. Stone with a surface (this slice)

- **Texture sets**, generated by `tools/textures/stone.py` (numpy; seamless by construction, fixed seeds):
  - **Slab**, for walls and pillars: brutalist cast stone. Faint pitting, weathering streaks, the odd spalled patch, very few seams.
  - **Flags**, for floors: irregular flagstones with worn edges and dark joints, each flag a little different in tone and level.
  - **Rubble**, for rubble floors and wall tops (the cut section): packed broken chunks.
- **Each set is two textures, 512 px, covering 4 m:**
  - a packed surface map: tone variation, roughness, cavity and height;
  - a normal map.
  - They ship as WebP, adding about 1 MB to the single-file build.
- **Mapping:** triplanar in the shared stone shader (no UVs needed). The surface map modulates the layer's stone colour, so each layer tints the same stone. Normals are blended per axis. Stage 1's mottling stays on top to hide the tiling.
- **Water:**
  - Puddles fill the low parts of the stone first (the joints between flags), using the height channel.
  - Under water the surface goes flat and glossy (roughness about 0.12).
  - Wet specular gets its own ceiling, higher than dry stone's but still under the bloom threshold. The torch lays a clear glancing streak, and glow lights throw coloured glints across puddles.
  - Wet stone darkens more, so puddles read against textured stone.
- **Darks:** raise each layer's `fillIntensity` until room edges are just readable. Camouflage is re-measured at the same seed.
- **Budget:** texture sizes are halved on Low quality. Record the provenance in technical design §9 (original, script-generated).

**Checks**
- Unit tests: the texture script's outputs are the committed files; the maps are seamless (wrap test).
- Smoke: existing brightness checks hold. New: water glint is visible (a puddle under the torch is brighter than dry stone beside it) but below white.
- Look: before/after pairs at the same seed.

**Done means**
- [ ] The torch rakes across stone: you can see pitting, joints and edges in the pool
- [ ] Puddles read: darker, flat, and a clear glancing streak from the torch; glints from glow plants
- [ ] Room edges are just readable outside the beam; a dead torch is still near black; camouflage holds
- [ ] Stone stays under the ceiling; nothing but emissives blooms

## 2b. Walls that aren't boxes (next slice, outline)

A modular kit built by scripts in `tools/blender/` (headless Blender through the `bpy` module, in its own virtualenv), exported as `.glb` with rendered previews committed:

- **Face panels** for each open side of a wall tile: intact, chamfered, spalled, cracked, collapsed top.
- **Corner caps** for outside corners.
- **Pillars:** intact, spalled, with a missing chunk.
- **Door frames:** the header block and its strip.

Rules for the kit:
- Pieces stay inside the tile footprint, so collision and pathfinding don't change.
- Pieces are authored so the cutaway's sink still works.
- One `InstancedMesh` per variant, within the draw-call budget.
- Variants are picked per tile from the campaign seed.
- The full-height shadow proxies stay authoritative (rule 9).

Joins:
- rubble chunks along wall bases;
- uneven flagstones (visual only);
- crack decals.
