# Sound shortlist: recordings for the sound pass

Download from **freesound.org** with the licence filter set to **Creative Commons 0** (left-hand filters, "License"). CC0 needs no credit, and keeping the Freesound ID in each filename lets us trace everything anyway.

**How to hand them over:** make one folder, e.g. `sounds-raw`, with a subfolder per section below. Then either connect that folder to this chat in the Claude desktop app, or zip it and attach it.

**What to pick:**
- The uncompressed download (WAV, FLAC or AIFF), not the MP3 preview.
- Clean, close recordings over long field recordings with traffic or voices.
- Three to six of each is plenty. I'll pitch, stretch, layer and reverse them, and deal them out per campaign, so no two runs sound the same.

## 1. Clicks: something communicating
Plant screams and calls, the "what was that" layer.
- `dolphin clicks`, `echolocation clicks`
- `snapping shrimp`
- `crab clicking`, `crab claws`
- `beetle stridulation`, `insect stridulation`
- `bone rattle`, `wooden clicks` (dry, close)
- `cicada` (slowed right down, it becomes something else)

## 2. Chitter: birds and small things
- `starling chatter`, `bird chatter close`
- `swift scream` (the bird)
- `bat social calls` (these are ultrasonic recordings slowed down)
- `squirrel chatter`, `monkey chatter`
- `insect chirp`

## 3. Howls and groans: low, long, far off
- `humpback whale song`, `whale moan`
- `wolf howl` (single animals, not packs with people)
- `elephant seal`, `walrus`
- `ship hull creak`, `wood creak`, `tree creaking in wind`
- `metal groan`, `bowed metal` (for the Warden machines later)
- `didgeridoo drone` (slowed, it becomes a throat)

## 4. Wet and vegetal: plants moving and being cut
- `squelch`, `mud bubbles`, `slime`
- `celery snap`, `cabbage tear`, `lettuce crunch` (foley for cutting growth)
- `leaves rustle`, `bush rustle`, `branch snap`
- `vine rip`, `roots tearing`, `peeling`
- `wet footsteps mud`

## 5. Water
- `water drop cave`, `single drip`
- `drip puddle`, `drip bucket` (a few different pitches)
- `cave ambience drips`
- `underwater bubbles`
- `trickle stone`, `water running wall`

## 6. Rooms and air
- `cave room tone`, `cave ambience` (no voices, no drips if possible)
- `wind cave`, `wind through gap`
- `distant rumble`, `earth rumble`

## Nice to have
- `footsteps stone`, `footsteps gravel`, `footsteps puddle`
- `gear rustle`, `backpack rustle`
- `breathing gas mask`, `respirator breathing`
